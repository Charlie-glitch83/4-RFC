# No Tuning and Bold Claims

The project does not become bolder by weakening the firewall. It becomes bolder by deriving and executing enough that direct claims are justified.

## Clean claim examples

- `RFC derives X from the frozen triadic premises under assumptions A.`
- `RFC generates state Y without empirical fitting.`
- `The N-body structure follows from the enhanced kernel at the declared finite-N scope.`
- `This frozen observable survived the preregistered blind comparison.`

## Forbidden claim jumps

- formalized law -> physically executed state;
- representative check -> complete universe;
- internal closure -> empirical validation;
- one domain example -> all manifestations;
- successful fit after target-driven repair -> prediction.

## Target-blind repair test

Before testing a repair against a known failure, record:

- internal defect;
- source and parent;
- derived replacement;
- unchanged scientific definitions;
- frozen tests and tolerances;
- withheld consequences.

Passing the old regression shows repair. Passing independent withheld consequences supports generality.
