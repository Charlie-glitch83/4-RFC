# Part VII — Scientific design rules that prevent another false finish

## 34. A law is not a state

A theorem that an operator is positive, conservative, or convergent does not produce the universe-specific input on which the next module must operate.

## 35. A state schema is not a state

A handoff listing 40 required fields is valuable, but those fields must contain generated arrays, graphs, events, values, units, covariance, provenance, and restart identity.

## 36. A representative check is not physical execution

Synthetic matrices and manufactured solutions verify implementations and identities. They cannot be promoted into the unique RFC universe branch.

## 37. An aggregate score is not closure

Every mandatory physical component must pass individually. A score of `0.997` cannot compensate for a missing solver, violated conservation law, absent covariance, or synthetic parent.

## 38. Conventional physics can be a limit, not a hidden generator

Newtonian, relativistic, kinetic, MHD, nuclear, atomic, radiative-transfer, and stellar equations may be used only under an explicit status:

```text
RFC-DERIVED
RFC-LIMIT/CORRESPONDENCE
TRANSPARENT INTERFACE
COMPARISON OBJECT
UNRESOLVED/RESERVED
```

Silently importing a standard solver and relabeling its state triadic would break the central claim.

## 39. Realism must come from fidelity, not targets

The route to a realistic universe is:

- better resolution;
- more complete generated species/processes;
- controlled approximations;
- overlap and refinement convergence;
- uncertainty propagation;
- independent implementation;
- later blind comparison.

It is not branch selection by resemblance to our observed universe.

## 40. The triad must do indispensable work

Every module needs a triad-necessity program. At minimum compare:

- full triadic specialization;
- CIF ablation;
- QV ablation;
- RFL/memory ablation;
- witness ablation;
- no-loss/reopening ablation;
- kernel-depth/route ablation;
- a comparably flexible non-triad alternative.

A triadic-origin claim is stronger when the full model has a distinctive cross-domain consequence that the ablations and alternatives cannot reproduce under equal freedom.
