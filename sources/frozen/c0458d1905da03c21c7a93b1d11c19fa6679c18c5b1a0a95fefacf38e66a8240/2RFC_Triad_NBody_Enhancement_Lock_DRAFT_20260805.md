## 6. Carrier lifecycle and activation

1. `PRESERVED_DORMANT` — inherited without affecting local physical output;
2. `GRAMMAR_ACTIVE` — witness, route, event, branch, memory, and no-loss grammar is used without claiming the N-body dynamical theorem solves the domain;
3. `DOMAIN_ACTIVE(N)` — a specialization theorem activates direct finite-relational dynamics;
4. `EVENT_ACTIVE` — event/branch logic is active on a witnessed boundary;
5. `PROMOTED_CONTINUUM` — a finite-relational state is represented as a field, distribution, mesh, or effective object with reopening evidence;
6. `LATENT` — manifested dynamics ended and qualified memory remains;

\[
\operatorname{Phys}(F_D(H_D,\mathfrak C^D))
=
\operatorname{Phys}(F_D(H_D,\varnothing))

## 7. Scope of N-body necessity

Triad necessity and N-body necessity are not identical tests.

### Universal necessity

Every admitted manifestation must preserve the indispensable roles of:

- CIF;
- QV;
- RFL;
- the First Action;
- inherited recursive structure;
- source identity and no-retune discipline.

### Grammar-active necessity

When the carrier is `GRAMMAR_ACTIVE`, tests must show that removing the declared relation, witness, event, branch, no-loss, memory, or provenance function breaks the corresponding protected property.

### Domain-active necessity

When the carrier is `DOMAIN_ACTIVE(N)`, tests must show that the specialized finite-relational dynamics performs indispensable physical work at the declared scope.

### Dormant rule

When the carrier is `PRESERVED_DORMANT`, removing it from the local physical equation must not change the local physical output. Its importance lies in preserved ancestry, future activation capacity, reopening, and global continuity—not hidden backreaction.

No module may be forced to show a direct Newtonian change from a carrier declared dormant.
