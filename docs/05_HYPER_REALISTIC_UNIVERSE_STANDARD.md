# Hyper-Realistic Universe Standard

A hyper-realistic universe is not a visually detailed toy. It is a causally integrated numerical universe with evidence that its major physical and observational products are stable, reconstructable, and source-derived.

## Required dimensions

- actual physical dynamics solved;
- cross-domain causal backreaction;
- conservation and constraint closure;
- resolution, volume, time-step, network, and solver convergence;
- full uncertainty and cross-domain covariance;
- branch, route, event, memory, and earliest-change replay;
- restart at material checkpoints;
- multiple resolutions and, at Gold Standard, ensembles;
- independent critical implementations;
- truth-level metric, Weyl, lightcones, lensing, radiation, transient, and multimessenger products;
- synthetic maps, spectra, time series, signals, catalogues, selection/window functions, and error models;
- frozen generation before comparison;
- blind multi-probe validation;
- exact source-to-observable lineage;
- reproducible environment and commands.
