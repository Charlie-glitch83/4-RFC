# 3-RFC Scientific Constitution

## Governing claim

3-RFC is built to establish that existence in its entirety emerges from the ordered triad

\[
\mathcal T=(\mathrm{CIF},\mathrm{QV},\mathrm{RFL}),
\qquad
\mathrm{QV}(\mathrm{CIF})\rightarrow\mathrm{RFL},
\]

and that this finite primitive structure admits an unbounded family of lawful manifestations. It does **not** assert that all manifestations obey one identical observable equation.

- **CIF — Cosmic Infinite Field:** admitted modal possibility and source capacity.
- **QV — Quantum Vacuum:** lawful compression, selection, crossing, action, or actualization.
- **RFL — Recursive Fractal Lattice:** stabilized, inheritable, memory-bearing manifestation.

The First Action is initially prephysical. The Big Implosion is the sole first physical event.

## Parent architecture

- Presentation 29 owns the source ontology, First Action, recursive kernel, domain-specific modal inheritance, memory, and no-retune architecture.
- The revised triadic N-body proof enhances the terminal kernel with constituted identities, directed relations, local witnesses, routes, gauge/multiroute classes, events, branches, conservation, no-loss memory, reopening, and controlled finite-N refinement.
- Presentation 30 owns the universe-scale causal order, handoffs, observer products, reproducibility, generation freeze, and later comparison architecture.

The N-body proof enhances the RFC universe. It neither replaces the universe nor turns every domain into point-body mechanics.

## Derivation rule

Each domain must derive its own state space, variables, dimensions, symmetries, governing action/evolution, constraints, coefficients, scales, observables, uncertainty, and falsifiers from admitted parents. Shared triadic ancestry is not permission to rename an imported equation.

If the admitted premises do not select a unique law, preserve the lawful branch family or obstruction. Never insert a familiar public answer to make the chain move.

## No tuning

Generation is sealed from public targets, best-fit values, remembered observations, calibrated public simulations, target-trained surrogates, curve fitting, post-hoc residual correction, branch shopping, seed shopping, and tolerance shopping.

A repair is lawful only when compelled by exact source interpretation, triadic descent, mathematics, dimensions, causality, conservation, covariance, branch completeness, numerical convergence, or a frozen internal falsifier.

## Failure rule

Preserve the evidence of old failures; do not preserve failed mechanisms or failed outputs as required parents. Old failures are regression tests and negative controls. The enhanced derivation is expected to repair them without using their target answers as fitting objectives.

## Claim rule

Claims must be as bold as the owning evidence allows, but no stronger. Formal derivation, implementation, representative verification, physical execution, independent reproduction, freezing, and empirical validation remain different states.
